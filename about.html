Insurance Advisor · Solapur

Kartik Poonamchand Ladda

Kartik founded Ladda Insurance with a simple promise: You Select, We Cover. From Homkar Nagar, Bhavani Peth, he advises families, salaried professionals, shop owners and manufacturers across Solapur on health, life, vehicle, fire, shop and marine insurance, and on long-term investing through mutual funds.

His approach is personal. He explains policies in plain language, compares plans from several insurers, and stays with clients through renewals and claims. That consistency has made him one of the top advisors in the National Insurance Pune Region.

Values
