FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Which insurance companies do you work with?

Kartik works with leading life, general and health insurers. These include LIC, National Insurance, New India Assurance, Star Health, Care Health, Tata AIG and more. He compares plans across them so you get the best fit, not just one company's product.

Do you help at the time of a claim?

Yes. Claim support is the most important part of the service. Kartik helps with documents, cashless approvals, surveys and follow-ups until the claim is settled.

Is there any extra charge for your advice?

No. You pay the premium charged by the insurance company. Consultation and comparison are free.

Can I buy a policy without visiting the office?

Yes. Most policies can be completed on WhatsApp with e-KYC and online payment. Home or shop visits are also available within Solapur.

Do you help with mutual fund SIPs?

Yes. Kartik helps you plan goal-based SIPs, lumpsum investments and SWPs for regular income. Mutual funds are subject to market risks.

Not sure which plan is right for you?

Get honest advice from Kartik. Compare plans from leading insurers with no pressure and no hidden charges.
