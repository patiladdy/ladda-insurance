Term Insurance: The Biggest Protection at the Lowest Cost

By Kartik Ladda · 6 min read · Updated October 2026

Term insurance is the purest form of life insurance. If the insured person dies during the policy term, the family receives the full sum assured. Because there's no savings component, premiums are very low. A healthy 30-year-old can often get ₹1 crore cover for roughly the cost of a monthly mobile and OTT bill.

Who needs it?

  • Anyone whose family depends on their income
  • People with home, car or business loans
  • Parents planning children's education
  • Business owners who want to protect the family from business debts

How much cover?

A common thumb rule is 10–15× your annual income, plus outstanding loans and future goals, minus existing savings. Use our Term Cover Calculator for a needs-based figure.

Useful riders

RiderWhat it does
Accidental death benefitExtra payout if death is due to an accident
Critical illnessLump sum on diagnosis of listed serious illnesses
Waiver of premiumFuture premiums waived on disability or critical illness
Accidental disabilityIncome or lump sum on permanent disability

Mistakes to avoid

  1. Hiding medical history or smoking habits. This can lead to claim rejection.
  2. Buying too little cover to save premium.
  3. Choosing a term that ends too early. Cover until at least age 60–65.
  4. Not telling your nominee about the policy.
Premiums for individual life insurance policies are exempt from GST since September 2025.
