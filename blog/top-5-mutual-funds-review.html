Top 5 Mutual Funds Review: Category-wise Picks for Long-term SIPs

By Kartik Ladda · 9 min read · Updated October 2026

Choosing a mutual fund isn't about picking last year's top performer. It's about building a portfolio across categories that matches your goal, time horizon and comfort with risk. Below are five widely followed funds, one from each core equity category, that investors often consider for long-term SIPs.

Important: This is an educational review, not a recommendation. Fund names, fund managers, expense ratios and portfolios change over time. Always check the latest factsheet and Scheme Information Document, and talk to your advisor before investing. Past performance does not guarantee future returns.

1

Parag Parikh Flexi Cap Fund

Flexi capValue-oriented

It's known for a disciplined, value-oriented style and a relatively compact portfolio. It has historically held some international stocks alongside Indian equities, and the fund house is known for transparent communication with investors.

✓ Why investors like it
  • Diversified across market caps
  • Long, consistent investment philosophy
  • Good as a single "core" fund
✗ Keep in mind
  • May lag in sharp momentum rallies
  • Overseas exposure depends on regulatory limits
2

ICICI Prudential Large Cap Fund

Large capTop-100 companies

This is one of the largest large-cap funds by assets. It invests mainly in India's top 100 companies, which tend to fall less than mid and small caps in market corrections.

✓ Why investors like it
  • Relative stability within equity
  • Large, experienced fund house
  • Good anchor for any portfolio
✗ Keep in mind
  • Large caps can be hard to beat. Compare with index funds.
3

HDFC Mid Cap Fund

Mid capGrowth

It's one of the largest and oldest mid-cap funds. Mid-sized companies can become tomorrow's large caps, which gives higher growth potential than large caps, with more ups and downs along the way.

✓ Why investors like it
  • Long track record across market cycles
  • Well-diversified mid-cap portfolio
✗ Keep in mind
  • Can fall sharply in corrections
  • Needs a 7+ year view
4

Nippon India Small Cap Fund

Small capHigh risk-high growth

It's among the largest small-cap funds, with a very widely diversified portfolio of smaller companies. It's best used as a satellite holding (10–20% of the portfolio) for long horizons.

✓ Why investors like it
  • High long-term growth potential
  • Broad diversification reduces single-stock risk
✗ Keep in mind
  • The most volatile category
  • SIP is better than lumpsum here
5

HDFC Balanced Advantage Fund

HybridDynamic asset allocation

It moves between equity and debt based on market conditions. That gives a smoother ride than pure equity, so it suits first-time investors, retirees and SWP-based income.

✓ Why investors like it
  • Lower volatility
  • Equity taxation with a hybrid approach
  • Good for SWP
✗ Keep in mind
  • Returns usually lower than pure equity in bull markets

Taxation in brief

For equity-oriented funds, gains on units held for more than 12 months are long-term capital gains. They're taxed at 12.5% above an annual exemption of ₹1.25 lakh. Short-term gains are taxed at 20%. Rules can change, so check with your tax advisor.
