Fire, Shop & Factory Insurance: A Guide for Solapur Businesses

By Kartik Ladda · 6 min read · Updated October 2026

Solapur is known for its textile industry: terry towels, chaddars and powerlooms, as well as engineering workshops, beedi units and thousands of retail shops. A single fire or short circuit can destroy years of hard work. Business insurance is the safety net that keeps your enterprise alive.

Perils usually covered under fire policies

Fire, lightning, explosion, riot and strike, storm, flood and inundation, earthquake (often as an add-on), landslide, bush fire, impact damage and missile testing, depending on the policy wording.

Common mistakes

  • Under-insurance: if you insure stock worth ₹50 lakh for only ₹25 lakh, claims are reduced proportionately.
  • Not declaring stock fluctuation. Use a floater or declaration policy.
  • Ignoring add-ons like earthquake, terrorism and loss of profit.
  • Poor record keeping. Keep bills, stock registers and photographs.
