Vehicle Insurance Explained: Third-party vs Comprehensive & Must-have Add-ons

By Kartik Ladda · 5 min read · Updated October 2026

Under the Motor Vehicles Act, third-party insurance is mandatory for every vehicle on Indian roads. But third-party cover pays only for damage to others. For damage to your own car or bike, you need comprehensive or own-damage cover.

Third-partyComprehensive
Injury/death of third party✓✓
Third-party property damage✓✓
Own vehicle accident damage✗✓
Theft, fire, flood✗✓
Personal accident cover for ownerMandatory add-onMandatory add-on

Add-ons worth considering

  • Zero depreciation: full claim for parts without a depreciation deduction. Best for new vehicles.
  • Engine protection: covers water-ingression damage, which is useful during the monsoon.
  • Return to invoice: pays the invoice value on total loss or theft.
  • Roadside assistance: towing, battery jump-start and flat tyre help.
  • NCB protector: keep your no-claim bonus after a small claim.

Smart renewal tips

  1. Renew before expiry to keep your NCB and avoid inspection.
  2. Transfer your NCB when you sell your old vehicle and buy a new one.
  3. Don't claim for very small damages if it would cost you a higher NCB.
  4. Compare IDV (insured declared value), not just premium.
