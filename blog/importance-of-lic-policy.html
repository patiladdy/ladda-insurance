Life & LIC

Why an LIC Policy Still Matters: Benefits Explained

By Kartik Ladda · 6 min read · Updated October 2026

For generations, an LIC policy has been the first financial product in most Indian households. Many people now ask whether traditional plans still make sense when term insurance and mutual funds are available. The honest answer is that they serve a different purpose, and for many families that purpose still matters.

Key benefits of LIC traditional plans

  • Sovereign-backed trust. LIC is a Government of India–owned insurer. The government guarantee on policy money is a major source of comfort for conservative savers.
  • Forced, disciplined savings. A fixed annual premium builds a savings habit, which suits people with irregular or business income.
  • Life cover + savings in one. Endowment and whole-life plans pay a death benefit during the term and a maturity benefit with bonuses at the end.
  • Bonus additions. Participating plans earn reversionary bonuses each year and a possible final additional bonus at maturity. These are declared by LIC and are not guaranteed in advance.
  • Loan facility. After a few years you can take a loan against the policy at reasonable rates instead of breaking investments.
  • Tax benefits. Under the old regime, premiums may qualify under Section 80C, and maturity proceeds may be tax-free under Section 10(10D), subject to conditions.
  • Nomination & settlement network. LIC has a branch and agent network in every district, including Solapur.

Where LIC traditional plans fit and where they don't

NeedBest tool
High life cover for family income protectionTerm insurance (LIC or private)
Guaranteed/low-risk savings with life coverLIC endowment / whole-life plans
Long-term wealth creation (10+ yrs)Equity mutual funds via SIP
Regular pension after retirementLIC pension/annuity plans or SWP
Traditional plans usually give moderate returns (check the real yield with our Policy IRR Calculator). The smartest approach for most families is term insurance for protection + LIC for safe savings + SIP for growth.

Tips before you buy

  1. First buy adequate term cover (10–15× your annual income).
  2. Choose a premium you can comfortably pay for the full term. Surrendering early causes losses.
  3. Always ask for the official benefit illustration.
  4. Keep nominee and bank details updated.
