How SIPs Grow Wealth: The Power of Compounding

By Kartik Ladda · 5 min read · Updated October 2026

A Systematic Investment Plan (SIP) lets you invest a fixed amount in a mutual fund every month, starting from as little as ₹500. It's the simplest way for salaried people, shop owners and even students to build long-term wealth.

Why SIP works

  • Compounding: returns earn further returns. Time matters more than the amount.
  • Rupee-cost averaging: you buy more units when markets fall and fewer when they rise, which averages out your cost.
  • Discipline: an automatic debit removes the temptation to time the market.
  • Flexibility: increase, pause or stop the SIP at any time (except ELSS lock-in).
