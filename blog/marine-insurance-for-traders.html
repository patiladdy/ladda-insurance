Marine Insurance: Protecting Goods in Transit for Traders & Manufacturers

By Kartik Ladda · 5 min read · Updated October 2026

"Marine" insurance isn't only for ships. It covers any goods in transit by road, rail, sea, air or courier. For Solapur's textile exporters, agro traders and engineering units, a single truck accident or theft can mean a loss of lakhs.

Types of marine cargo policies

  • Single transit policy: covers one consignment.
  • Open policy / open cover: covers all shipments over a year, up to a limit, with declarations.
  • Annual turnover policy: premium based on yearly turnover. Easy for regular dispatches.
  • Inland, import and export cover as needed.

Coverage options

Institute Cargo Clauses (A) give all-risk cover with standard exclusions. Clauses (B) and (C) cover named perils at lower cost. Add-ons like theft, pilferage and non-delivery (TPND), and strike, riot and civil commotion (SRCC) can be included.

Claim tips

  1. Note damage on the lorry receipt or delivery challan.
  2. Inform the insurer immediately and preserve damaged goods for survey.
  3. Keep invoices, packing lists and transport documents.
  4. Lodge a monetary claim with the carrier within the time limit.
