Top LIC Policies: Which Plan Suits Your Goal?

By Kartik Ladda · 8 min read · Updated October 2026

LIC offers dozens of plans, and choosing the right one can be confusing. Below is a goal-wise review of the most popular LIC plans people ask us about in Solapur. Plan names, features and availability can change, so always confirm the latest version and benefit illustration before you buy.

1

New Jeevan Anand: the classic all-rounder

ParticipatingWhole-life cover

It pays the sum assured plus bonuses at maturity, and life cover continues for the whole of life after that. That's why it's a favourite for people who want both savings and lasting protection.

✓ Strengths
  • Two benefits: maturity and lifelong cover
  • Bonus participation
  • Loan facility
✗ Consider
  • Moderate returns
  • Higher premium than term for the same cover
2

Jeevan Labh: pay less, stay covered longer

Limited payEndowment

The premium-paying term is shorter than the policy term. This suits business owners and salaried people who want to finish paying during their earning years.

✓ Strengths
  • Limited premium years
  • Lump sum at maturity
  • Bonuses
✗ Consider
  • Premiums are higher during the paying years
  • Lock-in until maturity
3

Jeevan Umang: income for life

Whole lifeSurvival benefits

After the premium-paying term, it pays an annual survival benefit for life, plus a lump sum at maturity or on death. It's popular as a "pension-like" plan for the family.

✓ Strengths
  • Regular income
  • Covers up to age 100
  • Legacy for children
✗ Consider
  • Long commitment
  • Inflation reduces the real value of a fixed income
4

Jeevan Lakshya: securing your child's goal

Child goalFamily income

If the policyholder dies, future premiums are effectively not required, the family gets an annual income, and the maturity amount is still paid on time for the child's education or marriage.

✓ Strengths
  • Goal is protected in any case
  • Annual income to the family
✗ Consider
  • Equity SIPs may grow faster for long horizons
5

Jeevan Utsav: guaranteed lifelong income

Guaranteed additionsWhole life

It offers guaranteed additions during the premium term and income options afterwards. It suits risk-averse investors who value certainty over high returns.

✓ Strengths
  • Guaranteed benefits
  • Flexible income options
✗ Consider
  • Returns are modest, so compare the IRR

How to choose

  1. Protection first: buy a term plan for 10–15× your income.
  2. Pick the goal: child (Jeevan Lakshya), lifelong income (Umang/Utsav), savings + cover (Anand/Labh), pension (Jeevan Shanti).
  3. Check the real return with our Policy IRR Calculator and estimate maturity with the LIC Maturity Estimator.
  4. Ask for the official illustration before signing.
