Why Health Insurance Is a Must for Every Solapur Family

By Kartik Ladda · 6 min read · Updated October 2026

A single hospital stay for a heart procedure, dengue complications or a knee replacement can easily cost ₹2–6 lakh at a good private hospital, even in Solapur. Most families pay this from savings, by borrowing, or by selling gold. Health insurance stops one medical emergency from wiping out years of savings.

5 reasons you can't postpone health insurance

  1. Medical inflation is high. Hospital costs rise faster than general inflation. A ₹5 lakh cover that feels enough today may cover far less in 10 years.
  2. Lifestyle diseases start early. Diabetes, blood pressure and thyroid problems now appear in people in their 30s. Once you're diagnosed, buying a policy becomes harder and costlier.
  3. Waiting periods are shorter if you start early. Pre-existing diseases are covered only after a waiting period. Starting young means the waiting period is over before you need it.
  4. Cashless treatment. At network hospitals the insurer settles the bill directly, so you don't have to arrange money in an emergency.
  5. Tax benefit. Under the old tax regime, Section 80D allows deductions for health premiums paid for yourself, your family and your parents. The limits are higher for senior citizens.

What to check before buying

  • Room rent limits. A cap can reduce your whole claim proportionately. Prefer "no room rent capping" or "single private room".
  • Co-payment. Avoid it unless the premium saving is large.
  • Waiting periods for pre-existing diseases and specific illnesses.
  • Restoration benefit and no-claim bonus.
  • Network hospitals in Solapur where you'd actually go for treatment.
  • Claim settlement experience of the insurer.
Good news: since September 2025, premiums for individual health insurance policies are exempt from GST, which makes cover more affordable than before.

Bottom line

Health insurance isn't an expense. It protects everything else you've saved. Buy it while you're healthy, review it every few years, and add a super top-up as costs rise.
